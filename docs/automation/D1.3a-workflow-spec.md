## 4. What This Workflow May NOT Do (hard constraints, unchanged from `RULES.md`)
- Must not name any Maharashtra district hospital, medical board, or welfare-office contact (none is Reviewed yet — `docs/SOURCES.md` Open Gaps).
- Must not store chat ID, name, phone, address, Aadhaar, or free-text health data anywhere (Sheet or session state beyond the run).
- Must not make a medical, legal, or eligibility determination (`RULES.md` §6).
- Must not be published, activated, executed, or credential-configured by this specification alone.

## 5. Fixed Fail-Safe Message — SINGLE AUTHORITATIVE DRAFT (PROPOSED, not yet CEO-approved verbatim)
**This section is the ONLY place this exact string is authored.** Every other file (`gemini-prompt.md`, `n8n-build-manifest.md`, `Architecture.md`) must reference it by pointer ("see `D1.3a-workflow-spec.md` §5") and must never restate a different wording — three divergent fail-safe strings existed across the docs before this revision (`REVIEW-FINDINGS.md` item 12); this is the fix.

`docs/SOURCES.md` and `design.md` both reference "the fixed fail-safe message" / "could not verify notice" conceptually (design.md line 48, 76), but no canonical document currently fixes its exact characters. Per `PRD.md` FR-10 ("say exactly what could not be verified and point to the official UDID portal or district office"), this session proposes the following exact string for CEO/Product Owner sign-off — **flagged as proposed, not canonical, until approved and copied verbatim into `design.md`/`PRD.md`:**

> "I could not verify this specific detail (district hospital, medical board, or local welfare-office contact) from an official source. Please check the official UDID portal (swavlambancard.gov.in) or your district social welfare office directly. You are doing the right thing. Take it one step at a time."

Note the mandatory closing line (`PRD.md` FR-6) is included verbatim at the end, since FR-6 requires it on every *completed* guidance response and the fail-safe is a form of completed response (it resolves the user's question, even though the answer is "could not verify").
