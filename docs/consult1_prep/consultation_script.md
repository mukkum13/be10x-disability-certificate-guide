## The five questions

**1. Walk me through what actually happened the last time you (or someone you know) applied for a Disability Certificate or UDID card — where did the process get confusing or slow?**
*(Goal: find the real pain point, not a guessed one.)*

**2. If you'd had a tool that only answers using verified official-government information — and clearly says "I don't know, check the official portal" instead of guessing when it isn't sure — would that have changed anything for you? How?**
*(Goal: test whether the "grounded, fail-safe" design actually matches a real need.)*

**3. We currently only have confidently verified information for the national process and Maharashtra state-level escalation contacts — not district-level hospital/medical-board details. Is that gap a dealbreaker, or still useful as a starting point?**
*(Goal: honest signal on whether partial coverage is still valuable — do not lead the answer.)*

**4. Would you trust a Telegram bot for something like this, or would you want it on a website instead? Why?**
*(Goal: validate/challenge the channel choice.)*

**5. Is there anything about how we're describing this tool, or asking these questions, that feels off, insensitive, or unclear to you as someone closer to this experience than we are?**
*(Goal: catch tone/framing problems we can't see ourselves.)*
