# Code of Conduct

Contributors are expected to:

- Be respectful, professional, and constructive.
- Welcome people with different backgrounds, abilities, identities, and levels of experience.
- Focus feedback on the work and explain concerns clearly.
- Protect privacy by using dummy data and avoiding unnecessary personal or health information.
- Follow the project's scope, disclosures, and documented evidence.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate privacy violations, and disruptive bad-faith behavior are not acceptable. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when needed to keep collaboration safe and productive.
