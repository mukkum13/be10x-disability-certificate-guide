# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants are expected to communicate professionally, respectfully, and constructively. Assume good intent, focus on the work, and make room for different levels of experience and perspective.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, unwanted contact, and sharing private or sensitive information. Do not submit real applicant data, credentials, or other secrets.

## Project boundaries

Keep discussion relevant to this project. Do not use project channels to diagnose a person, decide eligibility, provide legal advice, or request personal health or identity information.

## Reporting concerns

Report conduct concerns privately to **mukku.m13@gmail.com**. Include only the information needed to understand the concern and do not forward sensitive personal data.
